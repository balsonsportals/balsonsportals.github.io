## 📱 Overview

The **BALSONS Mobile & Tablet Portals Suite** delivers an ergonomically engineered, touch-first mobile interface tailored specifically for onsite rigging engineers, lifting supervisors, crane operators, and HSE stewards working on the factory floor and outdoor laydown yards.

### 🌟 Core Mobile & Tablet Innovations
1. **Thumb-Zone Bottom App Bar:** Fixed navigation with 48px+ touch targets positioned within natural thumb reach for one-handed smartphone operation.
2. **Adaptive Tablet Split-Pane:** On screens $\ge 768\text{ px}$ (iPads and Android tablets), the layout automatically transitions to an ergonomic left rail navigation.
3. **Mobile 360° Dossiers with Touch Punchlists:** Bottom sheets slide up smoothly with interactive milestone check-offs, live progress calculation, and digital certificate generation.
4. **Fast Simulated Barcode/QR Scanner:** Immediate Form 11 lifting tackle certificate validity lookup on the go.
5. **Turnstile Gate Decision Engine:** Tri-portal interlock (HR KYC + Safety Form 31) tested in real-time on mobile.
6. **Instant Role Switcher & PIN Pad:** 1-tap fast shift handover between 12 authorized enterprise accounts with persistent session state.
7. **Bilingual Engine:** Rapid English & Hindi (हिन्दी) toggling for storekeepers and riggers.
8. **Seamless Desktop $\leftrightarrow$ Mobile View Switcher:** Instant transition buttons connecting mobile views to comprehensive desktop workspaces.
