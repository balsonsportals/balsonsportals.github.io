⚖️ BALSONS Dual Workforce Compensation & Statutory Framework:

Corporate Payroll Rulebook
1. Monthly Basis Employees (With PF & ESIC):
Monthly CTC basis. Payable days include Present (P), Weekly Off (WO), and Paid Leave (PL). Subject to mandatory statutory contributions: Provident Fund (12% of Basic, max ₹1,800), ESIC (0.75% of Gross), and Professional Tax (₹200). Generates comprehensive Corporate Salary Slip.
2. Daily Pay Employees (Without PF & ESIC):
Daily Wage Rate (₹ / Day) basis. Payable days equal Present (P) Days worked only (Weekly Off unpaid). Strictly Exempt from ESIC and PF deductions. Overtime computed on daily hourly rate (Daily Rate / 8h × 2). Generates statutory Daily Wage Slip.
