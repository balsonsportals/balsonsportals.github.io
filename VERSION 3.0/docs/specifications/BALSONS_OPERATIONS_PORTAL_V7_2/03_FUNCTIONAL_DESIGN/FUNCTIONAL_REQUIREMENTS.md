# FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION
**Document ID:** `BAL-OPS-FRS-001`  
**Governing Standard:** BAL-OPS-E2E-007 V7.2  

### 1. Functional Scope Overview
The Operations Portal provides end-to-end execution governance for heavy lifting, transport, and mechanical assembly across JTRWF Khordha.

### 2. Core Functional Modules
1. **Workspace Management:** Partitioning into `LINE-AXLE`, `LINE-WHEEL`, and `AREA-MACHINE-SHOP-AXLE`.
2. **Work-Package Lifecycle Engine:** 8 formal states with strict prerequisite checks and exit evidence.
3. **Scope Control Module:** Included 7 mechanical items enforced; 7 excluded items blocked unless varied.
4. **Manpower Planning & Allocation:** 8 lifecycle states tracked per person-to-work-package assignment.
5. **Lifting Resource Coordination:** Verification of Store Form 11 tackle certificates and crane utilization logs.
6. **Readiness Evaluation Gate:** Multi-point checklist evaluating drawings, foundation, route, tools, and PTW.
7. **Daily Site Execution & Shift Logbook:** Planned vs actual progress, measurable quantities, shift handover.
8. **Mechanical Completion Dossier:** Levelling and alignment inspection sign-off and punch point resolution.
