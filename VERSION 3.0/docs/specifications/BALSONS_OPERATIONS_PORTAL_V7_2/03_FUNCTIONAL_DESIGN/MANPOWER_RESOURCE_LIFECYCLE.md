# MANPOWER & RESOURCE LIFECYCLE SPECIFICATION
**Governing Standard:** BAL-OPS-E2E-007 V7.2 (§10 & §11)  

### 1. Eight Manpower Lifecycle States
The manpower lifecycle governs operational deployment. It is strictly **assignment-specific** (linking an employee reference to a specific work package, shift, and date range):

$$\text{REQUIRED} \longrightarrow \text{AVAILABLE} \longrightarrow \text{QUALIFIED} \longrightarrow \text{CLEARED} \longrightarrow \text{ALLOCATED} \longrightarrow \text{MOBILIZED} \longrightarrow \text{DEPLOYED} \longrightarrow \text{RELEASED}$$

1. **`REQUIRED`:** Manpower demand generated by workspace, work package, trade, and shift.
2. **`AVAILABLE`:** Candidate identified on site; pending qualification and clearance checks.
3. **`QUALIFIED`:** Trade/skill competency certificate verified against HR reference.
4. **`CLEARED`:** Safety induction, medical fitness, height pass, and gate access valid.
5. **`ALLOCATED`:** Formally assigned to a named work package, shift, and date range.
6. **`MOBILIZED`:** Reported to the assembly bay with gate muster confirmed.
7. **`DEPLOYED`:** Actively executing on the shop floor; confirmed by Area Supervisor.
8. **`RELEASED`:** Shift/task completed; release date/time and reason recorded.

### 2. Historical Schuler Forge Shop Baseline
- **Wheel Forging Line:** 1,075 Man-Days (9,675 hours at 9 h/MD).
- **Axle Forging Line:** 717 Man-Days (6,453 hours at 9 h/MD).
- **Combined Forge Shop:** 1,792 Man-Days (16,128 hours) across 44 planning weeks (01-Oct-2026 to 29-Jul-2027).
- **Safeguard Rule:** The 9-hour conversion is a derived assumption. It must not be used for payroll or billing without explicit project director sign-off.
- **Machine Shop Rule:** **Machine Shop has ZERO historical baseline.** All Machine Shop manpower must be planned from first principles.
