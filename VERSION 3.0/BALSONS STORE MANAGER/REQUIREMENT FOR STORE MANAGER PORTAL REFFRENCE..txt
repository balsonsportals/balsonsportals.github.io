# 1. DOCUMENT PURPOSE

This document defines the complete business, functional, operational and technical requirements for the BALSONS STORE MANAGER.

The system shall provide a centralised workspace for managing project inventory, tools and tackles, procurement, vendor quotations, purchase orders, material receipts, stock movements, maintenance, QR/barcode identification and management reporting.

This document shall serve as the authoritative reference for:

- System architecture and development.
- Inventory and stock management.
- Tool and equipment identification.
- Procurement and purchase management.
- Vendor management.
- Material receipt and issue workflows.
- Maintenance and repair tracking.
- Reports and registers.
- User roles and permissions.
- Audit, backup and recovery.
- Testing, acceptance and deployment.

The existing approved Excel master database shall be treated as the primary business reference wherever applicable. Its existing sheet names, structure, formulas, mappings, relationships and workflows shall be preserved unless a change is specifically approved.

**No unauthorised redesign of the master database or business workflow shall be permitted.**

# 2. PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Develop a professional BALSONS-branded Store Management System that:

1. Maintains accurate project inventory.
2. Provides real-time stock visibility.
3. Controls receipt, issue, return and transfer of materials.
4. Maintains a complete tool and equipment master.
5. Provides unique QR/barcode identification for tools.
6. Manages purchase requests and procurement.
7. Maintains vendor and supplier information.
8. Records vendor enquiries and quotations.
9. Supports purchase orders and delivery tracking.
10. Tracks maintenance, repair and calibration requirements.
11. Identifies shortages, low stock and overdue actions.
12. Generates professional inventory and procurement reports.
13. Maintains user accountability and transaction history.
14. Provides controlled Excel import/export and backup.
15. Minimises manual registers and duplicate data entry.

# 3. PROJECT SCOPE AND OPERATING MODEL

## 3.1 Operating Scope

The system shall operate on a **single-project basis**.

The system shall not introduce:

- Multi-project inventory management.
- Central-store and sub-store hierarchy.
- Company-wide procurement management.
- Multi-project stock transfers.
- Enterprise-wide asset management.
- Unapproved multi-project consolidation.

All inventory, procurement, vendor transactions, material movements and reports shall be associated with the configured project.

## 3.2 Business Coverage

The system shall cover:

- Consumables.
- Tools and tackles.
- Lifting equipment.
- Hydraulic equipment.
- Mechanical tools.
- Electrical tools.
- Measuring instruments.
- Safety-related equipment.
- Spare parts.
- Materials and accessories.
- Repairable tools and equipment.
- Other project-specific inventory.

## 3.3 Core Operating Cycle

**Requirement → Purchase Request → Vendor Enquiry → Quotation → Approval → Purchase Order → Material Receipt → Inspection → Stock Entry → Issue → Return / Consumption → Maintenance / Closure**

The workflow shall be configurable according to the authorised purchasing and store procedures.

Required functions:

- Record ordered quantity.

**Delivery Received → Quantity Verification → Inspection → Acceptance / Rejection → GRN → Stock Update**

Rejected or damaged material shall not be added to available stock.

- Consumable material.
- Returnable tools.
- Reusable equipment.
- Repairable equipment.

Stock balances shall be updated through controlled transactions.

A return shall not automatically restore an item to Available status if inspection or repair is required.

## MODULE 08 – TOOL AND EQUIPMENT MANAGEMENT

Purpose: Maintain the dedicated tool and equipment master.

This module shall use the approved BALSONS tool tagging standard.

### 8.1 Approved Tool Identification Format

**BAL-[2-character section]-[3-character tool type]-[3-digit serial]**

Example:

**BAL-LF-HYJ-001**

Electrical section code shall be `ELE`, not `EL`.

### 8.2 Locked Tool Inventory Baseline

| HT | 4 |
| **Total** | **264** |

### 8.3 Mandatory Tool Tag Fields

The tag shall contain:

- BALSONS branding.
- Physical Tag ID.
- Tool Type.
- QR Code.
- Key Specification.
- Make.
- Model.
- Serial Number.
- Status.
- Inspection Due Date or Calibration Due Date, where applicable.

Inspection and calibration fields shall be omitted where they do not apply.

The tag shall contain only the approved Level 1 and Level 2 information.

The tag shall not include project, location, employee, issue or return information.

### 8.4 Tool Status

Approved operational statuses:

- Available.
- Issued.
- Under Repair.
- Inactive.
- Retired.

Compliance status shall be calculated separately from operational status.

The compliance engine shall support the five computed compliance states and the approved `DATE_NOT_SET` handling.

The due-soon threshold shall be 30 days. No hard-coded default due date shall be introduced.

### 8.5 QR Tag Generator

Required functions:

- Select tools from the master register.
- Generate unique QR codes.
- Generate physical tag IDs.
- Automatically arrange tags on A4 sheets.
- Display QR code, ID and tool type.
- Print tags.
- Export print-ready PDF.
- Prevent duplicate tag IDs.
- Support reprinting with existing identity.
- Maintain tag generation history.

The QR code shall resolve to the relevant tool identity or controlled record lookup. It shall not expose confidential data without authentication.

# 8. COMMON INVENTORY AND TRANSACTION RULES

## 8.1 Stock Calculation

Stock shall be calculated through controlled transactions.

The system shall maintain the distinction between:

- Opening balance.
- Receipts.
- Accepted quantity.
- Issues.
- Returns.
- Consumption.
- Rejections.
- Adjustments.
- Closing balance.

A user shall not be able to manipulate closing stock without an authorised transaction or adjustment record.

## 8.2 Transaction References

The system shall generate unique references for transactions.

Proposed examples:

- PR-00001 – Purchase Request.
- RFQ-00001 – Vendor Enquiry.
- PO-00001 – Purchase Order.
- GRN-00001 – Goods Receipt.
- MI-00001 – Material Issue.
- MR-00001 – Material Return.
- MT-00001 – Maintenance.
- BAL-LF-HYJ-001 – Physical Tool Tag.

Final numbering formats shall be configurable.

## 8.3 Data Integrity

The system shall:

- Prevent duplicate master IDs.
- Validate mandatory fields.
- Validate numeric quantities.
- Maintain item references across transactions.
- Prevent invalid stock balances.
- Preserve historical transactions.
- Identify incomplete records.
- Validate Excel imports before committing changes.

# 9. EXCEL IMPORT AND EXPORT REQUIREMENTS

The existing approved Excel workbook shall remain the primary business reference.

The import engine shall:

1. Read workbook sheets.
2. Validate required sheet names.
3. Validate column headers.
4. Preserve existing mappings.
5. Check duplicate identifiers.
6. Validate data types.
7. Identify missing mandatory values.
8. Display an import summary.
9. Report rejected records.
10. Commit only validated records.

The export engine shall preserve required column structures and produce clean Excel-compatible reports.

The application shall not silently rename sheets, move columns, replace formulas or modify existing business mappings.
