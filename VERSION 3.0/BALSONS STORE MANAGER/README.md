## 🏗️ Overview

The **BALSONS Store Manager** is a mission-critical heavy rigging inventory, statutory compliance, material inward, and tool logistics system designed specifically for the Jupiter Tatravagonka Railwheel Factory (JTRWF) Schuler 10,000T heavy press erection project.

It unifies tool management across 8 rigging disciplines, maintains statutory Form 11 lifting gear registers under the Factories Act 1948 / Odisha Factories Rules 1950, enforces tool loan authorisation linked to safety-inducted personnel, provides Red-Tag safety quarantine tracking, generates Returnable/Non-Returnable Gate Passes (RGP/NRGP), and renders printable A4 QR asset tags.

---

## 🛠️ Key Capabilities

1. **264-Tool Inventory Database across 8 Rigging Sections:**
   - `LF` — Lifting & Rigging Gear (Slings, Shackles, Turnbuckles, Spreader Beams): 41 items
   - `PT` — Pulling & Tensioning Equipment (Chain Pulleys, Tirfors, Lever Hoists): 25 items
   - `MT` — Machine Moving & Skidding (Hilman Rollers, Skates, Hydraulic Toe Jacks): 13 items
   - `HF` — Hand & Impact Tools (Sloggers, Impact Sockets, Torque Multipliers): 155 items
   - `CC` — Clamping & Rigging Connections (Beam Clamps, Eye Bolts, Swivels): 6 items
   - `ELE` — Electrical & Temporary Rigging Power (Distribution Boxes, Extension Reels): 11 items
   - `SF` — Safety Equipment & Height Access (Tripods, Retractable Fall Arresters): 9 items
   - `HT` — Heavy Rigging Specialty & Hydraulic Tooling (700 bar pumps, Torque Wrenches): 4 items
   - **Total:** 264 Heavy Rigging Tools

2. **Statutory Form 11 Lifting Tackles & Gear Register:**
   - 53 statutory lifting items tracked under Factories Act 1948 (Section 29) & Odisha Factories Rules 1950 (Rule 55).
   - Tracks Safe Working Load (SWL), Safe Working Angle, Proof Load Test Date, Competent Person Certificate Number, Re-examination Due Date, and 30-day "Due Soon" automatic alerts.

3. **Tool Loans & Issue Desk:**
   - Digital issuance to personnel verified against Safety Induction (`EMP-2026-XXXX`).
   - Expected Return Date enforcement, condition on return (`OK` vs `DAMAGED`), and real-time stock balance updates.

4. **Damaged Equipment & Safety Quarantine Desk:**
   - Physical Red-Tag Tracking (`RED-TAG-2026-XXX`) for items failing pre-use inspection.
   - Defect categorization (Strand cuts, elongation, thread strip, gauge failure).
   - Statutory lock preventing quarantined gear from being loaned or issued until rectified and certified.

5. **Material Inward Register (MRN / GRN):**
   - Inward consignments, vendor PO references, physical vs challan count verification, QA inspection status (`ACCEPTED`, `REJECTED`, `PARTIAL`).

6. **RGP / NRGP Material Gate Pass Studio:**
   - Formal A4 Returnable & Non-Returnable Material Gate Pass challans with print preview and instant printing.
   - Authorized sign-off zones for Store In-charge, Site In-charge, and Main Security Gate.

7. **Printable QR Asset Tag Generator:**
   - Generates high-density A4 2x4 tag sheets.
   - Includes Tool Serial ID, Description, WLL / SWL rating, Location, and statutory inspection validity.

8. **Universal BEDS V1.0 Architecture & 360° Workforce Dossier:**
   - Integrated with BEDS Enterprise Workspace topbar.
   - Bi-directional sync with Google Sheet `1SyOGt6HEu6HJCufAbB2_P6BgF6pEppt28J_8cARaOP0`.
   - 360° cross-portal dossier verification for personnel authorizations, active tool loans, and safety records.
   - 100% Mobile & Smartphone Responsive with off-canvas slide drawer navigation.
